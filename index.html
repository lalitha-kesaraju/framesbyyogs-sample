The Design Guide

Everything Frames by Yogs makes, what each piece includes, and how ordering works — her full guide, on one page.

Photo Resin Frames

Your photos sealed in clear, glossy resin — a frame that never fades, yellows or breaks like glass.

  • Styles: round photo frames, heart crystal frames, ocean & beach themes, floral resin art
  • Wedding keepsake frames: your wedding photos with the day's treasures — garland, flowers and details — preserved in a wooden frame
  • Personalisation: names, dates and short quotes added in vinyl
  • Best for: weddings, anniversaries, couple gifts, home decor

Baby Keepsakes & Hand Casting

Tiny hands and tiny feet grow so fast. These pieces hold the first-year memories exactly as they were.

  • Baby photo resin clock: twelve months of baby photos in one keepsake clock
  • Baby details frame: birth details and first treasures, preserved together
  • Baby keepsake keychains: small mementos of the newborn days
  • Hand & foot casting: NEW — launched November 2026, bookings now open
  • Best for: newborns, first birthdays, baby showers

Custom Gifts

Small, personal and made one at a time — gifts that carry a photo, a name or a memory.

  • Engraved photo keychain: your favourite portrait, carved in wood
  • Alphabet resin keychains: letters and names in bright resin
  • Resin earrings & mini pins: light, handmade accessories
  • Best for: birthdays, return gifts, small celebrations

Vinyl Stickers & Sticker Sheets

Custom-cut vinyl lettering and sticker sheets, made on her own cutting machine.

About Yogalakshmi

Yogalakshmi Thiyagarajan is a resin artist and keepsake maker from Karaikal, India — and the hands behind every Frames by Yogs piece.

Her promise is in her own words: "save your precious memories." Wedding photos and garlands, a baby's first year, a family's favourite portrait — she preserves the moments people never want to lose, in resin frames, keepsakes and custom gifts.

In November 2026 she launched her newest offering — baby hand & foot casting — so those tiny hands can be held forever, long after they've grown. Bookings are now open.

Every order is handmade by her, one piece at a time — which is why she takes bookings 15 days ahead, and why no two pieces are ever exactly alike.

Quick Answers

The questions customers ask most. For anything else, send the enquiry form — it goes straight to Yogalakshmi's WhatsApp, and she replies herself.

How early should I book?

At least 15 days ahead of your date. Every piece is handcrafted from scratch, so prior booking is important — the earlier you book, the more relaxed the making can be.

Can I use my own photo?

Yes — that's exactly what the frames and keepsakes are made from. Send your favourite photo on WhatsApp or through the enquiry form, and it will be preserved in your piece.

How much does a piece cost?

Price depends on the size, your photos and the detailing the design needs, so there is no fixed price list. Share your idea on WhatsApp and Yogalakshmi will give you an exact quote before you confirm anything.

How long will my piece take?

Each order is poured, detailed and finished by hand, one at a time. That's why bookings are taken 15 days ahead — book at least 15 days before your occasion and your piece will be ready for your day.

How do I order?

Fill in the enquiry form or message on WhatsApp with your occasion, your date and the design you like. Yogalakshmi confirms the details and your exact quote personally — then your booking is in.
